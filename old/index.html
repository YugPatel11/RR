Mathematical Framework

How We Defined Pressure: The Ball-Level Stress Architecture

Pressure in modern T20 cricket is not a binary label. We quantify it on every single delivery via a continuous Pressure Index (PI ∈ [0.0, 1.0]), capturing chasing demands, par-score deficits, sudden collapses, and phase leverage.

Multi-Factor Stress Vectors

Chasing State (Innings 2): Measures Required Run Rate strain relative to historical baseline, velocity demand (runs needed per legal ball left), resource depletion ratio (balls used × wickets lost), and an acute crisis multiplier (RRR > 10.0 with ≤ 4 wickets in hand).

Setting Target (Innings 1): Evaluates scoring deficit against empirical par curves derived from 105,000+ historical first-innings balls per (Over, Wickets) state, and flags batting collapse situations (2+ wickets in the preceding 18 legal balls).

Match Context: Applies progressive death-overs leverage (overs 17–20), knockout multipliers (qualifiers, eliminators, finals), and close finish adjustments (≤ 15 runs or ≤ 3 wickets).

CHASE STRESS: PI_chase = 0.30·norm(RRR) + 0.20·norm(RPB) + 0.10·(Balls% × Wkts%) + 0.10·Crisis
DEFICIT STRESS: PI_inn1 = 0.25·Deficit_Par(Over, Wkts) + 0.15·Collapse(2+ in 18b)
LEVERAGE: + 0.10·DeathOver(17-20) + 0.05·Knockout + 0.10·CloseMargin

Methodology, Exclusion Rules & Cited Sources

Every statistic on this page is derived from reproducible code run against the official data pack. All external roster and contract facts are sourced and stamped.

Dataset Reconciliation & Exclusion Audit

Raw Ball Rows: 200,999 in ball_by_ball.csv across 867 fixtures.
Exclusion 1 (Super Overs): 38 balls from innings 3 and 4 were removed as single-over tiebreaks follow distinct resource rules.
Exclusion 2 (No-Result / Abandoned Matches): 1,753 balls from 24 abandoned or no-result fixtures were excluded because win-probabilities and leverage cannot be evaluated without a decisive outcome.
Net Analyzed Volume: Exactly 199,208 deliveries across 843 decisive matches (200,999 − 38 − 1,753 = 199,208; 867 − 24 = 843).

Section 1: Theoretical Methodology

The Ball-Level Pressure Stress Equation

Pressure is quantified ball-by-ball on a continuous scale PI ∈ [0, 1] combining chasing urgency, deficit vs par, collapse state, and death leverage:

• Chasing Stress: 0.30·RRR_norm + 0.20·RPB_norm + 0.10·(BallsElapsed × WktsLost) + 0.10·Crisis(RRR > 10 & ≤ 4 wkts)
• Setting Par: 0.25·Par_Deficit(Over, Wkts) + 0.15·CollapseState(2+ wkts in 18b)
• Leverage Multipliers: 0.10·DeathOvers(17-20) + 0.05·Knockouts + 0.10·CloseFinishMargin

Threshold: The top 25% deliveries (PI ≥ 0.231, N = 49,802) define our high-pressure regime.
