How We Defined Pressure

Pressure in T20 cricket is quantified using a multi-factor Pressure Index (0.0 to 1.0) calibrated across every delivery. In chases, it captures Required Run Rate stress, balls remaining, wickets in hand, and crisis thresholds (RRR > 10 with ≤ 4 wickets). In setting totals, it measures scoring deficit relative to empirical par-score curves, sudden batting collapses (2+ wickets in 18 balls), and death-over leverage. High-stakes knockout matches and close margins further elevate ball leverage, establishing the top 25% highest-stress deliveries as our high-pressure regime.

Rajasthan Royals War Room: IPL 2027 Auction Fit

RR Tactical Objectives & Roster Archetypes

Roster Context: ₹157 Cr team purse cap; 8 overseas maximum. Indian core solidified by Jaiswal, Parag, Jurel, and phenom Sooryavanshi, with Archer and Curran providing multi-phase value.

Primary Auction Targets: (1) Specialized death-overs pacer with proven yorker/deception metrics. (2) High-tempo middle-order finisher. (3) Economical wicket-taking spin controller. (4) Domestic Indian pace depth.

Methodology Summary & Technical Limitations

Data Scope: 199,208 legal and extra balls spanning 843 competitive fixtures across IPL (2022–2026) and T20I (2022–2026). Super Overs excluded; rain-shortened matches evaluated using revised targets and overs.

Rating Architecture: Incorporates context-adjusted runs/economy above expectation, performance uplift, boundary/dot frequencies, and match-level consistency. Empirical Bayes shrinkage dampens low-sample outliers toward role priors.

Stability & Verification: Evaluated via 500 match-level bootstrap iterations to establish 90% empirical confidence intervals and Top 10 retention rates.

Key Limitations: (1) Ineligibility constraints: Pakistani bowlers (Muqeem, Abrar, Shaheen, Nawaz) excel in T20Is but remain unavailable for IPL auctions. (2) Retired players (Dinesh Karthik) must be filtered from auction strategy. (3) Opposition heterogeneity in bilateral T20Is requires ongoing strength adjustments. (4) Stadium boundary dimensions and pitch wear could not be isolated due to unstandardized venue metadata.
