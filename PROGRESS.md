### 3. Ruthless 6-Point Critique Re-Evaluation
1. **Squint Test:**
   - Dominant elements remaining legible: Display headline *"The players we can actually buy."*, the 3 unboxed metrics (`20`, `12`, `8`), the pink active filter button, and the vertical gold number spine.
   - Elements dropping back: Body copy, hairlines, table headers, and footnotes blur into clean horizontal scanning bands.
2. **Grayscale Test:**
   - Heading and metric numerals remain distinct in pure luminance.
   - Without pink, `12 ACTIONABLE` loses its chromatic prominence and matches the visual weight of `20` and `8`.
   - Table rows maintain clear separation due to high-contrast bone text on navy background.
3. **Remove-One-Effect Test:**
   - Removed the 3 summary card boxes, background fills, and border radii. Replaced with unboxed tabular numerals separated by hairlines.
   - Zero drop-shadows, zero glassmorphism, zero floating blur containers.
4. **Strict Gold Audit:**
   - `#D4AF37` is strictly isolated to `.rating-gold` (the composite Bayesian Pressure Rating score). 0% leakage elsewhere.
5. **Repetition Check:**
   - Table displays all 20 players in dense financial broadsheet format; mobile displays high-density scorecard cards.
6. **Editorial Quality & 3 Honest Weaknesses:**
   - *Weakness 1 (Table Density on Medium Screens):* 9 columns cause the valuation string to wrap across 3 lines on viewports under 1200px.
   - *Weakness 2 (Visual Weight Competition):* The 3rem unboxed numbers compete with the 2.6rem headline for immediate focal attention.
   - *Weakness 3 (Avatar Asymmetry):* Candidates lacking photographed face cutouts fall back to initials boxes, creating minor visual asymmetry in the candidate lockup.
